## Top findings for Cardinal
1. **ep04's death scene is faithful to White.** Checked line by line against ch. 29 (pp. 170–174): Room 120/122, the picnic menu, Mrs. Grinbaum, the cascara and bicarbonate, Heunisch, the three last utterances, and "ten minutes."
   - Dr. Humphris's full name is **Francis Howard Humphris** (testimony cited in n. 8), so the drafts' "Dr. Francis Humphris" stands.
2. **Inquest dates** (CANON-121):
   - Convened Mar 6 at the Moana; chemists testified Mar 7.
   - Verdict the evening of **Mar 9**, printed Mar 10 (ch. 36, pp. 205–211).
   - **Fixed** ep04 "inquest is three weeks away" → "six days away".
   - **Rekeyed** the ledger row 1905-03-01 → 1905-03-09 (placeholder note replaced) and its timeline-art key.
3. **Jordan's trip and the cover statement** (CANON-122):
   - Jordan sailed on the S.S. *Alameda* on Mar 4 with Hopkins, Callundan and Reynolds, and arrived Mar 10.
   - He engaged Dr. Ernest C. Waterhouse.
   - The Jordan–Hopkins statement ("advanced age… surfeit of unsuitable food… exposure of the picnic party") ran in the Hawaiian Star on Mar 15.
   - Hopkins: "we all wish it to be a death from natural causes, don't we?"
   - Jane had meant to fire both men on her return (p. 201).
   - **Fixed** chronology.md, which had an invented "Dr. Ernest Forbes," a "chartered steamer," a "heavy dinner," and a composite last-words quote.
4. **Estate suit and how the university survived** (CANON-123):
   - The court-allowed household allowance, an insurance policy, sales of personal belongings, and a 10% salary cut (ch. 10 ¶17, pp. 58–59).
   - This resolves the CANON-060 [needs source].
   - The drafts' "faculty declared her personal servants" is **not** in White and is now marked [needs source]. "Four hundred students" is marked too (White: 559 at the opening).
5. **Founding Grant, taxes, the 1900 amendment** (CANON-125):
   - The Grant came on Nov 11, 1885 under the March 1885 Enabling Act (ch. 9 p. 53).
   - It was not tax-exempt. Jane wanted a blanket exemption, and Crothers dropped it from the 1900 amendment (ch. 12 pp. 71–72).
   - **Fixed** chronology.md ("tax-exempt"; a Winkler cite that has no Founding Grant content).
6. **Chronology chapter cites corrected:** death of Leland Jr. → ch. 7; Founding Grant → ch. 9; estate suit → ch. 10; Jan 1905 poisoning → chs. 1–2; Honolulu → chs. 28–47.
