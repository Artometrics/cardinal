## Top findings for Cardinal
1. **Where "late May" comes from** (CANON-133):
   - Ambrose cites the "hardest blow" sentence to E. B. Crocker's letter of **May 27, 1867** (ch. 11 n. 34, p. 397).
   - His own narrative then follows letters of June 26 and 28 and has Charles confront the strikers on **July 1 "after a week"** (¶54–57). That fits Chang's Monday, June 24 start.
   - **Fixed** the documentary draft, which had credited Ambrose for the June 28 dating.
2. **Provisions** (CANON-134): Ambrose has Charles Crocker cutting off provisions (¶57). Chang has E. B. crediting the Chinese headmen at the time. The drafts already follow Chang. Logged as a dispute.
3. **Ten-mile day** (CANON-135; ch. 16 ¶36–49, pp. 347–350):
   - The $10,000 bet; the April 27 derailment postponed the attempt.
   - Sunrise 7:15 a.m. Corinne time; six miles by the 1:30 meal; ten miles fifty-six feet by 7 p.m.
   - Eight named Irish rail handlers; spikes "put in place by the Chinese."
   - **Fixed** ep02's "Seven in the morning, a whistle" (all copies). **Rewrote** the ledger 1869-04-28 note with the actual cite (it had said "EPUB not re-opened") and refined its framing.
4. **Promontory** (CANON-136; ch. 17 ¶40–50, pp. 363–365): the gold spike was tapped into a pre-drilled hole. "Stanford swung and missed, striking only the rail. It made no difference." The draft quotations (documentary l.1465, moments.md) are verbatim. This confirms CANON-091 against Rayner and Winkler.
5. **Smaller checks:**
   - Verified: CP incorporated June 28, 1861, and "combined wealth, *according to Huntington*, was $159,000" (ch. 3 ¶48). ep02 now attributes the figure to Huntington.
   - Verified: Crocker's pistol and "bold front" (ch. 2); "almost identical device to the Crédit Mobilier" (ch. 5 ¶18); Strobridge "I will not boss Chinese" (ch. 7 ¶21); six to twelve inches a day (ch. 9 ¶60); Mrs. Strobridge, "the only white woman" (ch. 14 ¶82); Malvern Hill, "a thousand dead and three thousand wounded" (ch. 3); the Lexington's 202 days with Sherman aboard (ch. 2).
   - ep02's "so cut off" corrected to Ambrose's "so isolated" (ch. 2 ¶44, p. 52).
